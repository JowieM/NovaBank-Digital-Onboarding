### 5. Project Purpose

The purpose of this project is to analyse the customer account-opening and onboarding process of a fictional banking organisation, NovaBank.

The project will identify business problems, analyse the current process, define business and system requirements, and propose a digital customer onboarding solution.

The proposed solution will allow customers to complete account-opening activities digitally while providing bank employees with tools to review, verify and process applications.

---

### 6. Business Problem

NovaBank currently relies heavily on branch-based and manual customer onboarding activities.

Customers may be required to visit a branch, complete forms, submit supporting documents and wait for bank employees to capture and verify their information.

This creates several business challenges:

- Manual data capture
- Duplicate data entry
- Paper-based processes
- Longer application processing times
- Limited application status visibility
- Increased administrative workload
- Difficulty monitoring application performance
- Increased risk of incomplete customer information

---

### 7. Project Objectives

The project aims to:

1. Analyse the existing customer onboarding process.
2. Identify business process inefficiencies.
3. Define business requirements for a digital onboarding solution.
4. Define functional and non-functional requirements.
5. Develop use cases for key system interactions.
6. Define customer and application data requirements.
7. Design an improved future-state process.
8. Define reporting and KPI requirements.
9. Define testing and user acceptance requirements.
