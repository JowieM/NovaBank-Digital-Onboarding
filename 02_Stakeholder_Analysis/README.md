# Stakeholder Analysis

## Overview

Stakeholder analysis was conducted to identify individuals and groups affected by, involved in, or able to influence the NovaBank Digital Customer Onboarding & Account Opening System.

The analysis considers each stakeholder's role, responsibilities, level of interest, level of influence and required engagement approach.

---

## Stakeholder Identification

The key stakeholder groups identified are:

- Customers
- Customer Service Agents
- KYC Analysts
- Branch Managers
- Compliance Officers
- Operations Managers
- IT Team
- Executive Management
- System Administrators

---

## Stakeholder Analysis Approach

Stakeholders were assessed using two primary dimensions:

### Interest

The degree to which the stakeholder is affected by or interested in the project.

### Influence

The stakeholder's ability to affect project decisions, requirements, implementation or outcomes.

---

## Power/Interest Analysis

Stakeholders were grouped into four engagement categories:

### Manage Closely

Stakeholders with high influence and high interest.

These stakeholders require frequent engagement and active participation.

### Keep Satisfied

Stakeholders with high influence but comparatively lower day-to-day interest.

These stakeholders should receive sufficient information to maintain support.

### Keep Informed

Stakeholders with lower influence but relevant interest in the project.

These stakeholders should receive regular project information.

### Monitor

Stakeholders with lower influence and lower interest.

Their involvement should be monitored in case their influence or interest changes.

---

## RACI Analysis

A RACI matrix was developed to clarify responsibilities for major project activities.

RACI represents:

- **R — Responsible**
- **A — Accountable**
- **C — Consulted**
- **I — Informed**

The RACI matrix helps prevent unclear ownership and overlapping responsibilities.

---

## Communication Planning

Communication requirements were defined based on stakeholder needs.

Different communication channels may include:

- Business workshops
- Technical workshops
- Management meetings
- Email
- Dashboards
- System notifications
- Customer portal notifications

The frequency of communication depends on the stakeholder's role and involvement.

## Key BA Insight

Stakeholder analysis ensures that requirements are not developed from a single perspective.

Different stakeholders interact with the onboarding process differently. Customers focus on usability and transparency, operational teams focus on efficient processing, compliance teams focus on verification and controls, while management requires visibility into performance and outcomes.

Understanding these differences helps the Business Analyst develop requirements that address the needs of the wider organisation.
