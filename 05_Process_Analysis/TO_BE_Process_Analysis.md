# TO-BE Process Analysis

## 1. Overview

The TO-BE process represents the proposed future-state customer onboarding and account-opening process for NovaBank.

The proposed process introduces digital capabilities to reduce manual activities, improve customer visibility and increase operational efficiency.

---

## 2. Proposed Solution

NovaBank will introduce a Digital Customer Onboarding & Account Opening System.

The proposed solution will allow customers to:

- Register online
- Complete an account application digitally
- Select an account type
- Capture personal information
- Upload supporting documents
- Submit an application
- Track application status
- Receive notifications

Bank employees will be able to:

- Review submitted applications
- Validate customer information
- Review supporting documents
- Perform KYC checks
- Request additional information
- Approve or reject applications
- Monitor application queues

---

## 3. TO-BE Process Flow

The proposed process follows these major steps:

1. Customer accesses the NovaBank digital onboarding platform.
2. Customer creates an account.
3. Customer verifies their contact details.
4. Customer selects the required bank account type.
5. Customer enters personal and contact information.
6. Customer uploads required supporting documents.
7. System validates that required information has been provided.
8. Application is submitted.
9. KYC Analyst reviews and verifies customer information.
10. KYC Analyst reviews supporting documentation.
11. Additional information may be requested if required.
12. Application is reviewed for approval.
13. Application is approved or rejected.
14. If approved, the banking system creates the customer account.
15. Application status is updated.
16. Customer receives an automated notification.
17. Customer can view the application status through the digital platform.

## 5. Proposed Process

### Customer Registration

The customer accesses the digital onboarding platform and creates a user profile.

### Digital Application

The customer selects an account type and completes the required information electronically.

### Document Upload

The customer uploads required supporting documents.

### Automated Validation

The system checks whether required fields and documents have been provided before allowing submission.

### KYC Verification

The KYC Analyst reviews customer information and supporting documentation.

### Application Review

The application is reviewed based on defined business rules and verification results.

### Decision

The application is either approved or rejected.

### Account Creation

For approved applications, the banking system creates the customer account.

### Notification

The customer receives an automated notification containing the application outcome and relevant next steps.

### Status Tracking

The customer can view the current application status through the digital platform.

## 8. Business Rules

The following preliminary business rules have been identified:

### BR-001
A customer must register before submitting an application.

### BR-002
Mandatory customer information must be completed before submission.

### BR-003
Required supporting documents must be uploaded before the application can be submitted.

### BR-004
An application must undergo KYC verification before final approval.

### BR-005
An application cannot be approved if mandatory verification requirements have not been satisfied.

### BR-006
Rejected applications must contain a recorded rejection reason.

### BR-007
Customers must be notified when the application status changes.

### BR-008
Only authorised employees may review or approve applications.

### BR-009
Application status changes must be recorded in an audit trail.

### BR-010
Customer information must only be accessible to authorised users.
